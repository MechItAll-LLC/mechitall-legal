Effective date: October 22, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 22, 2026
Applies to the Low Flying Ducks game (com.lowflyingducks.game), by MechItAll LLC

You can delete your Low Flying Ducks account and its data at any time. The fastest way is inside the game. If you can't open the game anymore, email us.

1. Delete in the game

  1. Open Low Flying Ducks.
  2. Tap the gear icon (Settings) on the main menu.
  3. Scroll to About and tap Delete my account and data, then confirm.

Deletion happens right away and the game closes; next time it starts fresh.

2. Request deletion without the game

Email contact@mechitall.com with the subject "Delete my Low Flying Ducks account" and include anything that helps us find it: your username, your friend code (shown on the Online screen), or the Google account email you signed in with. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours, and we'll complete the deletion within 30 days.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your account (anonymous or Google-linked)
  • Your leaderboard scores
  • Your public profile: username, country, friend code, rating and last-online time
  • Your friend list, and you from your friends' lists
  • Quick-match queue entries and race records you took part in
  • Your cloud save: coins, items, statistics, missions, achievements and streaks
  • Your notification token and purchase entitlements
  • All game data stored on your phone

4. What may be kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (product, order ID, date) for as long as tax and accounting law requires, generally up to 7 years, and for fraud prevention.
  • Backups held by Google may contain deleted data for a short time before they're overwritten.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel Duck Club or Season Pass in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Things you bought stay in your Google account and can be restored with Restore purchases if you play again.
