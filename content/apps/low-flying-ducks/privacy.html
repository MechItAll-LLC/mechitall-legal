Applies to the Low Flying Ducks game (com.lowflyingducks.game) and these web pages

Low Flying Ducks is an arcade game: you steer a tank and shoot down ducks (and aliens, bugs, parrots, dragons and aircraft) that drop things on you. You can play alone, on one phone with friends, against friends on nearby phones, or online.

In short: you can play without giving us your name, email or location. We keep what online features need (your chosen username, scores and game progress), we don't sell your data, we don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and you can delete everything from inside the game at any time.

1. Who we are

MechItAll LLC, Florida, USA ("we," "us") makes Low Flying Ducks and is the controller of the personal information described here. Contact: contact@mechitall.com. Postal address and registration details are in our legal notice.

3. What we don't collect

  • No location. The Bluetooth and location permissions for Nearby race are used only so Android can find the other phone; your location is never recorded or sent.
  • No advertising ID, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • Nearby races and nearby co-op exchange your username, score and health (and in co-op your tank's position and hits) directly between the two phones; that data doesn't go through our servers.

4. How we use it, and legal bases

We use the information above only to run the game's features (performance of our contract with you), to keep the game fair and secure, prevent cheating and fraud (legitimate interests), and to meet legal obligations such as keeping purchase records. We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or profile you.

5. What other players can see

Your username, country flag, scores (with theme, tank, difficulty and date) and Duck Club crown are visible to everyone on the leaderboards. Signed-in players can see your username, country, friend code, last-online time and rating if they have your friend code or you're friends. Your opponent in a race sees your username and your live score and health. A co-op teammate also sees your tank move and your hits land, and the team's score is listed under both usernames; it stays on the co-op leaderboard if only your teammate deletes their account, and is removed with the account of the teammate who posted it.

6. Who we share it with

We use Google as our service provider: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging and Hosting store and process the data above on our behalf, under Google's data processing terms. Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect players' safety. Otherwise we don't share personal information.

7. Purchases and subscriptions

Coin packs, premium packs and the Duck Club and Season Pass subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. Google tells us what you bought so we can deliver it; we check purchases with Google's servers. You manage and cancel subscriptions in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions).

8. Sponsor cards and links

The game shows small sponsor cards, served from inside the game without any ad network, and nothing about you is sent to sponsors. If you tap Open on the game-over screen, the game opens the sponsor's app, Google Play page or website, and their own privacy policy applies there.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete it right away (see Delete your account). Purchase records (product, order ID, date) are kept as long as needed for tax, accounting and fraud prevention, generally up to 7 years. Google's backups may hold deleted data for a short period before it's overwritten.

10. Your rights and controls

  • Delete: Settings → Delete my account and data deletes your account and everything listed above, online and on your phone.
  • Correct: change your username and country in Settings.
  • Notifications: turn invite notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Access and portability: email us for a copy of your data.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Brazil), you may have further rights, including to object or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any right, email contact@mechitall.com; we answer within 30 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights.

11. Children

Low Flying Ducks is meant for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Security and international transfers

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so players can only change their own data. Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries; where required we rely on Google's standard contractual clauses.

13. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the game if the change is significant.
