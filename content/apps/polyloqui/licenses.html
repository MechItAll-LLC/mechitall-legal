Polyloqui is built on open-source software, models and voices. Thank you to everyone who made them.

Speech recognition

  • Whisper (tiny, base, small, large-v3-turbo) by OpenAI, MIT licence. Converted for on-device use by sherpa-onnx and onnx-community.
  • Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 by NVIDIA, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).
  • Silero VAD by Silero Team, MIT licence.
  • TitaNet small by NVIDIA (NeMo), Apache License 2.0. In the app, optional: recognises the owner's voice and tells speakers apart.
  • sherpa-onnx by the k2-fsa project (Next-gen Kaldi), Apache License 2.0.

Translation

  • Google ML Kit on-device translation and language identification, under the Google APIs Terms of Service.
  • Opus-MT models by Helsinki-NLP (University of Helsinki), CC BY 4.0, converted by Xenova (web version).
  • M2M100 418M by Meta AI, MIT licence (web version, all-languages pack).
  • Transformers.js by Hugging Face, Apache License 2.0; ONNX Runtime Web by Microsoft, MIT licence (web version).
