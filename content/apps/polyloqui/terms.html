What Polyloqui is

Polyloqui lets people talk in voice rooms, see what is said as text, translate it, and use a phone as an interpreter. It is provided free of charge. Some features may later be offered as a paid upgrade; any price will be shown before you pay.

Using it responsibly

  • Everything said in a room is written down and shown to everyone in that room. Tell the people you talk with, and do not use Polyloqui to record or listen to anyone without their knowledge where the law requires their consent.
  • Do not use Polyloqui to harass, threaten or harm others, to share illegal content, or to break the law.
  • Do not try to disrupt the service, get around its limits, or use it to attack other systems.
  • Room hosts are responsible for who they let into their rooms and can mute or remove people.

Translations and transcripts can be wrong

Speech recognition and machine translation make mistakes, especially in noise, with accents, names or technical words. Do not rely on Polyloqui where a mistake could cause harm, for example for medical, legal, safety or emergency communication. Use a qualified interpreter for those.
