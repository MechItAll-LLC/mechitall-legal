Polyloqui has no accounts. Room data exists only while the room is open, and nothing about you is stored on our server, except reports, which we keep for up to 90 days after the report is dealt with (see the Polyloqui privacy section).

Delete what's on your phone

Your name, languages, saved phrases, room code and settings are stored only on your device. Delete them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. In the web version, clear the site's data in your browser.
