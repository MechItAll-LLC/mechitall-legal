Applies to the artograffi app (com.artograffi.app) and its web pages

artograffi lets you draw in 3D space over your camera view, tag the result to the place you made it, and share it publicly, with friends, or with no one. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights and controls you have.

In short: we collect what the features need, we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and you can delete your account and content at any time.

MechItAll LLC, Florida, USA ("we," "us") makes artograffi and is the controller of the personal information described here. Contact: contact@mechitall.com. This policy applies to the artograffi app and its web pages, not to other apps or sites we link to.

3. Location, specifically

  • When: the app asks for location the first time a feature needs it. You can allow it while using the app, allow it once, or decline. Location features stop working until you allow it, but the rest of the app still works.
  • Tagging: saving a piece stores the coordinates where you saved it. To show a friendly place name ("near Main Street"), those coordinates are sent once to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service and the resulting place name is stored with the piece. Our servers also send the coordinates (never your name or account) to Open-Meteo for the weather at that moment and to Wikipedia for the nearest landmark, and store both with the piece.
  • Event forecasts: an event's location and date are sent from our servers to Open-Meteo to show its weather forecast and sunset time.
  • Nearby: to find nearby art, hotspots, live sessions and events, your current location is sent to our servers, which return only what's within range. Who can see what is enforced on our servers.
  • Event address search: when you organize an event and search for an address, what you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find matching places.
  • Proximity alerts: if vault, community-event or audio-guide alerts are on, the app checks your position while it's open, and shows a small radar symbol on screen while it does. Only if you turn on "Keep watching in the background" (off by default) does it keep checking while the app is in the background; Android then requires an ongoing "Watching for nearby vaults & events" notification. We don't use the "Allow all the time" background location permission, and checking stops if you turn the alerts off or close the app.
  • We don't try to figure out your home address, and we don't use location for advertising.

4. Camera, microphone and media, specifically

  • AR drawing: the live camera feed is processed on your device (Google ARCore for tracking, MediaPipe for hand and body tracking). The live feed isn't uploaded; only what you save is.
  • Photos, bursts and videos you capture and choose to save are uploaded.
  • Trace image and Wireframe capture detect edges on your device and save the resulting strokes, not the source image.
  • Upload media uploads the files you pick from your device.
  • Microphone is used only while you record a voice message or an audio clip. The app doesn't listen in the background.
  • Voice messages up to 60 seconds are sent to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text after sending, and the transcript is stored with the message so everyone in the chat can read it.
  • QR scanning processes the camera image on your device.

5. Automated content review

To keep the community safe, some content is analyzed automatically after you save or post it:

  • Photos, thumbnails, previews, profile pictures and backgrounds, and photos sent in messages are sent to Google Cloud Vision (SafeSearch), and videos to Google Cloud Video Intelligence, which return likelihood scores for nudity, sexual and violent content.
  • Critiques (comments) and the text of messages, including photo captions, are sent to Google Cloud Natural Language text moderation, which returns categories such as "sexual," "derogatory" and "toxic." Messages are checked only to enforce our rules; the text isn't used for anything else.

Pictures of saved pieces are also described automatically: Google Cloud Vision labels what they show (for example "mural" or "dog") so pieces can be found by search, and Anthropic's Claude writes a one-sentence description for screen readers and, when you tap Suggest titles, title ideas. Anthropic doesn't use this content to train its models.

A flagged photo or video is hidden from others until an administrator reviews it, and the result (flagged, reason, decision) is stored with it. A flagged critique is removed, and a flagged message is replaced with a notice that it was removed. Google processes this content on our behalf to provide these services. You can ask a person to review any automated decision by emailing contact@mechitall.com.

6. Translation

  • Messages: translated entirely on your device with Google ML Kit. Message text isn't sent anywhere to be translated.
  • Critiques (comments): when you tap Translate, that comment's text is sent to Google Cloud Translation and the translation is returned to you. We don't store the translation.

12. Children

artograffi is only for people 16 and older, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone younger. When you first open the app we ask for your date of birth to check this. We don't store the date itself: we only record that you confirmed the minimum age, on your device and on your profile. If someone enters an age under 16, the app stays locked on that device. If you believe someone under 16 has given us information, contact contact@mechitall.com and we'll delete it.
