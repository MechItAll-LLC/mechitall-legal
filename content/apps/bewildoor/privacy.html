Effective date: November 1, 2026
Applies to the Bewildoor game (com.bewildoor.game) on Android, Windows and in web browsers, and these web pages

Bewildoor is a puzzle game: you arrange clues and walk through matching doors. You can play the campaign alone, compete on leaderboards, or race friends live.

In short: you never give us your real name, email or location. The game creates a random ID and stores your chosen name, scores, replays and race positions so online features work. These are visible to other players. The game shows ads on room floors, but they're pictures stored in the game: no ad network, advertising ID or tracking is involved. There are no analytics, we don't sell your data, and you can delete it from inside the game.

1. Who we are

MechItAll LLC, Florida, USA ("we," "us") makes Bewildoor and is the controller of the personal information described here. Contact: contact@mechitall.com. Our postal address and registration details are in our legal notice.

2. Information we collect

Online features send data automatically: finishing a level uploads your result, and starting a level or opening a leaderboard can create your online ID.

3. What we don't collect

  • No email address, phone number, real name or payment details.
  • No location. Your "position" means where your avatar is in the game's maze, not where you are.
  • No advertising ID, or analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No contacts, device photos, files, camera or microphone access. The game's "phone camera" only captures the game itself.
  • There's no chat.

4. How we use it, and legal bases

We use this information only to run the game's online features (performance of our contract with you) and to keep them fair and secure, including removing offensive names and cheating (legitimate interests). We don't sell or rent it, use it for advertising, or profile you.

5. What other players can see

Anyone playing online can see your player name, scores and online ID on the leaderboards, and download your ghost replay. Anyone with a room's code can see your name, position, finish status and time in that race. The game doesn't display online IDs, but they are part of the data it receives.

6. Who we share it with

Google is our service provider: Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database and Hosting store and process this data for us under Google's data processing terms. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect players' safety. We don't otherwise share personal information.

If you download the game from itch.io or Google Play, those stores handle your account and download under their own privacy policies.

7. Ads

Bewildoor shows ads as pictures on the floor of some rooms, marked "AD". They currently promote other apps made by MechItAll LLC. The pictures are stored inside the game and can't be tapped. The level results screen lists the ads you saw, with links to those apps. A link opens the app or its Google Play page, only if you choose it, and Google's privacy policy then applies to the Play page. Showing ads and counting them on the results screen sends no information to us or anyone else, and we don't use an ad network, an advertising ID, or personalized ads. If we change how ads work, we'll update this policy first.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your online ID, name, scores and replays until you delete them. Race room data has no automatic expiry yet: it stays until you delete your data or we clear old rooms. After deletion, Google removes account data from its backups within 180 days, according to its documentation. See Delete your data.

9. Your rights and controls

  • Delete: About → Delete my online data in the main menu, or the email route if you can't open the game.
  • Correct: change your name in the main menu. It updates the next time you set a new best or join a race.
  • Ghosts: the Ghosts button only controls whether you see replays. Your own best-time replay is still uploaded; deleting your data removes it.
  • Access and portability: email us for a copy of your data.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Brazil), you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any right, email contact@mechitall.com; we answer within 30 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights.

10. Children

Bewildoor is meant for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Security and international transfers

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so players can change only their own entries. Google may process data in the United States and other countries; where required, we rely on the standard contractual clauses in Google's terms.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the game if the change is significant.
