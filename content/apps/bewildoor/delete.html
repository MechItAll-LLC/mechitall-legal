Effective date: November 1, 2026
Applies to the Bewildoor game (com.bewildoor.game), by MechItAll LLC

You can delete your Bewildoor online account and its data at any time. The fastest way is inside the game. If you've uninstalled it or can't open it, email us.

1. Delete in the game

  1. Open Bewildoor.
  2. On the main menu, select About.
  3. Select Delete my online data, then press it again to confirm.

Deletion happens right away. If you use an online feature afterwards, the game creates a new, empty online ID.

2. Request deletion without the game

Email contact@mechitall.com with the subject "Delete my Bewildoor data". Accounts are anonymous, so tell us your player name and, if you can, the boards you played and roughly when. We may ask for more detail to make sure the entries are yours, and we'll finish within 30 days.

Use this route too if you played version 0.5 or earlier: the in-game button only removes what was uploaded from the next version on.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your online ID (anonymous account)
  • Your leaderboard entries: name, scores and dates
  • Your ghost replays
  • Your race entries, and any race rooms you hosted

4. What's kept, and for how long

  • On your device: level progress, personal bests, settings and your player name stay until you uninstall the game or clear its data. They were never uploaded as progress, and we can't access them.
  • Technical logs: according to Google's documentation, logged IP addresses are kept for a few weeks for sign-in and a few days for the database.
  • Backups: according to Google's documentation, deleted account data is removed from its backups within 180 days.
