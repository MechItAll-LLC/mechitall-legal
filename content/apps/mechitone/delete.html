You can delete your MechItOne account yourself, at any time.

Delete your MechItOne account

  1. Open your MechItOne account page and sign in.
  2. Scroll to Delete account and press Delete my MechItOne account, then confirm.

This deletes your MechItOne account (email, name, picture and sign-in methods), your list of connected apps and your devices list, right away.

It does not delete your accounts inside the apps. Each app keeps its own account and data; delete those in each app (see each app's "Delete account" page in the Legal Center).

Remove only part of it

  • One app: on your account page, under Connected apps, press Disconnect. Or press Unlink inside the app.
  • One device: under Your devices, press Remove.

Can't sign in?

Email {{email}} from the address of your MechItOne account and ask us to delete it. We reply within 30 days.
