Applies to MechItOne, MechItAll's optional shared account (the account pages, the MechItAll community and "Sign in with MechItOne" in our apps)

MechItOne is one optional account for MechItAll apps. It works like "Sign in with Google": it tells an app who you are, and nothing more. Each app keeps its own data, and nothing is shared between apps.

In short: MechItOne keeps your email, name and how you sign in, which apps you connected, and the devices you used it on (never your location). An app you sign in to receives your MechItOne ID, name, email address and picture. If you use the community, the public name you choose and what you post are public; your email never is. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data. You can delete your MechItOne account, and everything you posted, at any time.

3. What apps receive

When you choose "Sign in with MechItOne" in an app and press Continue, that app receives your MechItOne account ID, your name, your email address (and whether it is confirmed) and your picture, signed by MechItOne so the app can check it is genuine. An app never sees your password, your other connected apps or your devices. What each app keeps is described in that app's privacy policy in our Legal Center.

3a. What is public in the community

Everything you post in the community (threads, comments, app ideas) is public: anyone can read it at account.mechitall.com/community, the latest comments on each app are also shown on that app's page on mechitall.com, and search engines may index these pages. Each post shows your public name and when you posted it. Your email address, your real name from your account, your picture, your votes and your reports are never shown to other people. Vote counts are shown only as totals. Please don't post personal details about yourself or others.

4. What we don't collect

No location, no contacts, no advertising IDs, no analytics or tracking, and no payment details. We don't sell or share your information for advertising.

5. How we use it, and legal bases

We use this information only to provide MechItOne: to create and secure your account, sign you in to the apps you choose, show you your connected apps and devices, run the community (show posts, count votes, handle reports and moderation), and prevent abuse. We read the app ideas and comments people post to decide what to build and improve. Legal bases (EU/UK): performance of our contract with you (providing the account and community you asked for) and our legitimate interest in keeping accounts and the community secure.

6. Who we share it with

  • The apps you choose: only what is described in section 3, only when you sign in to them.
  • Google (Firebase Authentication): stores your account and handles passwords and Google sign-in, as our processor.
  • Cloudflare: runs MechItOne and stores connected apps, devices, sign-in codes and community posts, votes and reports, as our processor.
  • Everyone: what you post in the community and your public name, as described in section 3a.
  • Turbify: hosts mechitall.com, including the account.mechitall.com address, which passes each request on to MechItOne without storing it, as our processor.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

7. Cookies and storage

The MechItOne site sets one cookie, mia_dev, a random device ID used only for your devices list (kept for 2 years). Firebase keeps your sign-in in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. Neither is used for advertising or tracking.

8. How long we keep it

Your account, connected apps, devices and community posts are kept until you delete them. You can remove a device, disconnect an app, delete any of your posts or take back a vote at any time. Posts hidden by a moderator stay stored, but not shown, until the author's account is deleted, so we can handle appeals and repeated abuse. Sign-in codes are deleted within 5 minutes. Technical logs are kept for up to 7 days. When you delete your MechItOne account, everything listed in section 2 is deleted, including your public name, all your posts and threads (with the replies in threads you started), your votes and your reports.

9. Your rights and controls

On your MechItOne account page you can see your details, change your name, link or remove a Google sign-in, see and disconnect apps, see and remove devices, and delete your account. In the community you can change your public name, delete your posts and take back your votes (see Delete your account). Deleting MechItOne does not delete your accounts inside the apps; delete those in each app. You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data.

10. Children

MechItOne, including the community, is for people aged 13 and older. Apps with a higher minimum age keep it when you sign in with MechItOne.

11. Security and international transfers

Sign-ins use the OpenID Connect standard with PKCE, and the information apps receive is signed. Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries, with the safeguards described in the general part of this policy.

12. Changes

MechItOne is new. If we change what it collects, we will update this policy and its date before the change.
