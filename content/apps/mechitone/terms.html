Applies to MechItOne, MechItAll's optional shared account

MechItOne is a free, optional account that lets you sign in to MechItAll apps with one email address or Google account. These terms add what is specific to MechItOne to the general terms above.

1. What MechItOne is

MechItOne only confirms who you are to the MechItAll apps you choose. Each app keeps its own account, data and rules: signing in with MechItOne doesn't move or merge data between apps, and each app's own terms still apply inside that app.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 13, or older where an app requires it.
  • Use your own email address or Google account, keep your password private, and tell us if you think someone else used your account.
  • One person per account. Don't create accounts to get around a ban or to impersonate someone.

3. Linking and unlinking apps

You can link an app to MechItOne, unlink it, or disconnect it from your MechItOne account page at any time. Unlinking keeps your account in that app; you sign in there the app's usual way afterwards. An app may stop offering MechItOne sign-in; your account in that app stays.

4. Acceptable use

Don't try to break, overload or get around MechItOne's security, use other people's accounts, or automate sign-ups. We may suspend or delete accounts that do.

4a. The community

  • You need to confirm your email address (by opening the link we email you) before you can use your MechItOne account in our apps or the community; accounts that sign in with Google are already confirmed.
  • The MechItAll community (forums, chat rooms, comments on apps and projects, app ideas and votes, profiles and achievements) is open to signed-in MechItOne users with a confirmed email address. Achievements, points and titles have no money value and can't be bought, sold or transferred. Our Community guidelines apply to everything you post.
  • Choose a public name that isn't offensive, misleading or someone else's. Names that look like ours ("MechItAll", "admin", "moderator" and similar) are reserved.
  • You keep ownership of what you post. You let us show, store and display it in the community and on mechitall.com, for as long as it stays posted. You can delete your posts at any time.
  • App ideas: you can suggest an app or game for us to make. We may build something similar to an idea, or something based on it, without owing you anything, and other people may have had the same idea. Only post ideas you're happy to share.
  • An automatic filter refuses posts with hateful, sexual or threatening content, spam or personal details (email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses), and masks swear words. Posts reported by several people are hidden until a moderator checks them.
  • Moderators may hide or close posts and threads that break these terms or the guidelines, and we may suspend accounts that repeatedly do. Use "Report" on anything that breaks the rules, or email us to appeal a decision.
  • One vote per person per idea; don't use several accounts or automation to post or vote.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your MechItOne account at any time on your account page. This ends these MechItOne terms for you and deletes everything you posted in the community. Your accounts inside the apps stay until you delete them there.

6. Availability

MechItOne is provided free of charge and as is. We work to keep it running, but it may sometimes be unavailable. If MechItOne is ever discontinued, we will tell you in advance where we can, and each app will keep a way for you to reach your account.
