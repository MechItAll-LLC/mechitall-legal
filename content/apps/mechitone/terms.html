Applies to MechItOne, MechItAll's optional shared account

MechItOne is a free, optional account that lets you sign in to MechItAll apps with one email address or Google account. These terms add what is specific to MechItOne to the general terms above.

1. What MechItOne is

MechItOne only confirms who you are to the MechItAll apps you choose. Each app keeps its own account, data and rules: signing in with MechItOne doesn't move or merge data between apps, and each app's own terms still apply inside that app.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 13, or older where an app requires it.
  • Use your own email address or Google account, keep your password private, and tell us if you think someone else used your account.
  • One person per account. Don't create accounts to get around a ban or to impersonate someone.

3. Linking and unlinking apps

You can link an app to MechItOne, unlink it, or disconnect it from your MechItOne account page at any time. Unlinking keeps your account in that app; you sign in there the app's usual way afterwards. An app may stop offering MechItOne sign-in; your account in that app stays.

4. Acceptable use

Don't try to break, overload or get around MechItOne's security, use other people's accounts, or automate sign-ups. We may suspend or delete accounts that do.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your MechItOne account at any time on your account page. This ends these MechItOne terms for you. Your accounts inside the apps stay until you delete them there.

6. Availability

MechItOne is provided free of charge and as is. We work to keep it running, but it may sometimes be unavailable. If MechItOne is ever discontinued, we will tell you in advance where we can, and each app will keep a way for you to reach your account.
