Applies to the BGRC game on the web (bgrc-3530c.web.app), for Android (com.mechitall.bgrc), Windows, macOS and Linux, and these web pages

BGRC is a simulation game: bots live, mate, evolve and build kingdoms. You can play entirely offline. The optional online features (cloud saves, a shared gene pool, published kingdoms, a weekly challenge and multiplayer rooms) need a small amount of information, described here.

In short: you never have to give us your real name, email or location. Online, we keep the name you choose and the game data you send. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no purchases. You can delete everything from inside the game at any time.

1. Who we are

MechItAll LLC, Florida, USA ("we," "us") makes BGRC and is the controller of the personal information described here. Contact: contact@mechitall.com.

2. Information we collect

Nothing is sent anywhere until you open the 🌐 Online window or join a multiplayer room. Then:

3. What we don't collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising ID, ad networks, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • No purchases or payment information: the game is free.
  • No chat: players in a room see each other's names and in-game actions only.

4. How we use it, and legal bases

We use the information above only to provide the online features you choose to use (performance of our contract with you) and to keep them working, fair and secure, for example by letting anyone verify a challenge score (legitimate interests). We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or profile you.

5. What other players can see

Everything you share is public to every BGRC player: shared genomes, your published kingdom, and challenge scores with their replays, each with your player name. Players in a multiplayer room with you see your player name, whether you're online, and your kingdom and actions in the shared world. Your account ID is visible to other players' devices in rooms and shared items, but it is a random code that doesn't identify you. Your cloud saves, your Google email and your room list are private.

When another player brings your shared genome or kingdom into their world, that copy becomes part of their world and stays there even after you delete yours.

6. Who we share it with

We use Google as our service provider: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, the Realtime Database and Hosting store and process the data above on our behalf, under Google's data processing terms. The game's fonts come from Google Fonts. The downloadable apps are distributed through Google Play and GitHub, whose own privacy policies apply to downloading. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect players' safety. Otherwise we don't share personal information.

7. How long we keep it

Cloud saves, shared genomes, published kingdoms, scores and your profile stay until you delete them or your account. Multiplayer rooms are deleted when their last member leaves; a long-term room stays while at least one member remains. Google's backups may hold deleted data for a short period before it's overwritten.

8. Your rights and controls

  • Delete everything: 🌐 Online → Delete my online data removes your cloud saves, shared genomes, published kingdom, scores, room list, profile and account. See Delete your data.
  • Delete single items: the ✕ next to a save, a shared genome or your score, and Unpublish my kingdom.
  • Correct: Change name in the Online window.
  • Access and portability: load any cloud save and use Export, or email us for a copy of your data.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Brazil), you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any right, email contact@mechitall.com; we answer within 30 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights.

9. Children

BGRC is meant for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Security and international transfers

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and protected by access rules, so players can only change their own data. Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries; where required we rely on Google's standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the game if the change is significant.
