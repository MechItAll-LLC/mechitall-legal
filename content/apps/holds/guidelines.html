Part of the Terms of Service

Raiding someone's hideout is the game. Being cruel to the person behind it isn't. These rules apply to forum posts, group chat, adventurer, clan and dungeon names, and anything else you create.

1. Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, bullying, or encouraging self-harm
  • Hate against people for race, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or nationality
  • Sexual content of any kind, and anything sexualizing a minor, which we report to the authorities
  • Asking anyone's age, address, school, photos or contact details, or trying to move a player to another app or meet in person
  • Sharing anyone's personal details or real-world location, including your own
  • Scams, including trading game items for real money
  • Spam, or impersonating staff or other players
  • Cheating, modified clients, exploiting bugs, or attacking the servers

2. Filters

Language is masked automatically, and links, contact details, phone numbers and coordinates are refused. Filters miss things and sometimes catch innocent words. Getting around them on purpose breaks these rules.

3. Reporting and blocking

Press Report on any post. Posts reported by three different players are hidden until we review them. Press Block on a forum post to stop seeing that author on your device. You can also email contact@mechitall.com with the adventurer name and where you saw the problem. Reports are private.

4. What we do

We aim to review reports within 48 hours. Depending on how serious it is, we remove the content, stop you posting, reset progress or close your online access. Serious cases, especially involving children, go straight to removal and to the authorities. To appeal a decision, email us; a person will look at it.
