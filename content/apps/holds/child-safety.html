Effective date: November 2, 2026
Applies to H.O.L.D.S. — Heroes of Legendary Dungeons Sought (com.holds.game), by MechItAll LLC

We don't tolerate child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) or child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form. H.O.L.D.S. is for players aged 13 and over.

1. What's prohibited

Any content or behavior that sexualizes, grooms, exploits or endangers a child, including sharing, requesting or linking to such material, soliciting minors, and sextortion. Attempts to get a player's age, location, photos or contact details, or to move them off the game, are prohibited for everyone.

2. How the game is built to prevent it

  • There are no images, voice or video anywhere. Player text is limited to public city forums, group chat, and text messages between two friends.
  • Two players can only message each other after one has entered the other's friend code and the other has accepted. Nobody can be contacted out of the blue, and either side can remove or block the other at any time.
  • The server masks language and refuses links, contact details, phone numbers and coordinates before anything is stored.
  • Accounts hold no real names, contacts or location, and real-world location is never shown to anyone.
  • Every post can be reported; posts reported by three different players are hidden automatically until reviewed. Messages between friends go through the same filters, and reporting a conversation sends its recent messages to our moderators.

3. How to report

In the game, press Report on the post. Or email contact@mechitall.com with the subject "Child safety report", including the adventurer name and where you saw it. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. In the US you can also report to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.

4. What we do

We treat child safety reports first. We remove the content, close the account's online access, and preserve what the law requires. We report apparent CSAM to NCMEC and cooperate with law enforcement, as required by US law and the laws of other countries where we operate.
