Effective date: November 2, 2026
Applies to H.O.L.D.S. — Heroes of Legendary Dungeons Sought, by MechItAll LLC

You can delete your online account and its data at any time. The fastest way is inside the game. If you've uninstalled it or can't open it, email us.

1. Delete in the game

  1. Open H.O.L.D.S. and load your adventurer.
  2. Open Menu.
  3. Under Online, select Delete my online data, then press it again to confirm.

Deletion happens right away. If you play online afterwards, the game creates a new, empty online ID. If you linked Google, a page then opens where you confirm with Google to remove that sign-in record too. You can also open it any time: remove my Google link.

2. Request deletion without the game

Email contact@mechitall.com with the subject "Delete my H.O.L.D.S. data". Accounts are anonymous, so tell us your adventurer name and, if you can, your clan or a forum post you made. We may ask for more detail to make sure the data is yours, and we'll finish within 30 days.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your online ID (anonymous account) and, on the page above, your Google sign-in record
  • Your cloud save, and the record that links your Google account to your adventurer
  • Your public profile and board entries: name, level, gold, rank and arena ratings
  • Your online hideouts and published dungeons
  • Any purse you dropped that is still lying in the game world, and your record of purses picked up
  • Your real-world exploring record (the scrambled areas you discovered), your phone's push alert token, your trade offers, and your part in this and last week's community goals
  • Your notices, your hideout on show (if you put it up), your name in the "recently nearby" lists of game areas, your forum and chat posts, your friends list and friend code, your conversations with friends, raid replays, your places on limited skills and titles, your game event history and your raid log
  • Raid parties you lead; your place in the fellowship or clan you lead (leadership passes to the next member, or the group is removed if you were the last)

4. What's kept, and for how long

  • On your device: your adventurer save and settings stay until you uninstall the game or clear its data. We can't access them.
  • Other people's data: your name can remain in another group's member list until the group next changes, and in raid logs of hideouts you raided. Email us to have these removed.
  • Moderation reports: copies of reported posts are kept while they're needed to handle abuse.
  • Technical logs and backups: according to Google's documentation, logged IP addresses are kept for a few weeks at most, and deleted account data leaves its backups within 180 days. Cloudflare's database recovery history lasts 30 days.
