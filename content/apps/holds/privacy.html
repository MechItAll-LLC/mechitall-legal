Applies to H.O.L.D.S. — Heroes of Legendary Dungeons Sought, on Android, Windows and the web, and these web pages

H.O.L.D.S. is an open-world online RPG. You build a secret hideout, raid others, tame and breed monsters, and join clans.

In short: you don't have to give us your real name or email. Linking a Google account is optional. When you play online, the game creates a random ID and stores your adventurer's name, level, gold, rank and arena stats, your hideout and dungeon layouts, and what you post on the forums. Boards, forums and the Who's playing list (name and level, which you can turn off) are public to other players. The game doesn't use your real-world location. Group chat is visible to that group. There are no ads or analytics, we don't sell your data, and you can delete it from inside the game.

3. Real-world location

Real-world exploring is optional and off unless you switch it on (Menu, "Link my real-world exploring", Android app only). Only then does the game ask Android for your approximate location (never precise GPS location), and only while the game is open on screen, about every 15 minutes and when you come back to it. It never runs in the background.

Each position goes straight to our server in one request and nowhere else: it isn't saved on your device, logged or shown. The server reduces it to an area of about 11 km, keeps only a scrambled (salted hash) record of the areas you've discovered and of your usual area, and answers with game facts only: whether you found somewhere new, and whether you're moving too fast for encounters (no monsters appear above walking or cycling speed). The places in the game that a discovery opens are made up and the same for everyone who finds that area, so they say nothing about where anyone really is.

Nobody else, no other player and no third party, ever sees your real location or anything made from it. You can switch it off at any time, and "Delete my online data" removes the area record. Android also lets you remove the permission in its settings.

4. What we don't collect

  • No phone number or payment details. No email address or name unless you choose to link Google.
  • No precise location, and no location at all unless you switch on real-world exploring (see section 3). No contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising ID, ads, crash-reporting or third-party tracking tools. If you turn on "Help improve the game" (off by default), the game sends counts of a few milestones, such as how many tutorial steps were reached that day, and reports of errors in the game (the error text, where in the game it happened and the game version). Both are added to daily totals with no ID, name or device information attached.
  • Hostile language: forum posts, chat and messages between friends may be checked by a safety model (Llama Guard) on Cloudflare Workers AI, the same provider that runs the game server. Only the message text is checked, nothing else about you; Cloudflare doesn't use it for training. Messages it flags as harmful are refused.
  • Translation: when you tap Translate on a post or chat line, that message's text is sent to a language model on Cloudflare Workers AI and the translation is kept with the post so other readers get the same copy. Nothing else about you or the author is sent.
  • Reports: when someone reports a post or messages, the game may send its owner a notice that a report is waiting (how many are open). The notice never includes the message, names or anything else about the players.
  • Raid replays: if you raid another player's hideout, your route through it and your health along the way are kept for the owner to watch, along with your adventurer name, and they can raid you back within a week. Holders of the rarest titles and limited skills are listed by adventurer name.
  • Forums are public and group chat is visible to the whole group. Messages between friends are visible to the two of them, are stored on our server (the latest 50 per conversation) so they can be delivered, and are seen by moderators only if one of the two reports the conversation.

5. How we use it, and legal bases

We use this information only to run the game's online features (performance of our contract with you) and to keep them fair and safe, including filtering language, reviewing reports and removing cheating (legitimate interests). We don't sell or rent it, use it for advertising, or profile you.

6. What other players can see

Anyone playing online can see your adventurer name, level, gold, rank, equipped title, clan tag and arena ratings on the boards, and your forum posts. Your online ID is included with forum posts so that players can block an author; the game doesn't display it. Clan and party members see each other's names. Players in the same part of the game world see your adventurer name for a few minutes ("recently nearby") and can read area chat. Rare titles, limited skills and leading the weekly board are announced by adventurer name in world news. Anyone raiding your hideout or dungeon sees its layout and your adventurer name. Other players never see your real-world location or anything made from it. In live play, players near you in the game world see the monsters you're fighting together and their health. If you trade, your trading partner sees the offer. Clan territory shows which clan holds each part of the in-game map.

7. Who we share it with

Our service providers store and process this data for us under their data processing terms: Google (Firebase Authentication, including Google sign-in; Realtime Database; Hosting; Firebase Cloud Messaging for push alerts) and Cloudflare (Workers and D1, which run the game server and hold the online data). We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect players' safety. We don't otherwise share personal information. If you download the game from Google Play, Google handles your account and download under its own privacy policy.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your online data until you delete it. Moderation reports are kept while they are needed to review a post and handle repeat abuse, and can contain a copy of the reported text. Google's documentation says it keeps logged IP addresses for a few weeks at most, and removes deleted account data from backups within 180 days; Cloudflare's database keeps a 30-day point-in-time recovery history. See Delete your data.

9. Your rights and controls

  • Stop sharing: Menu → Online → turn off Play online.
  • Delete: Menu → Online → Delete my online data, or the email route. If you linked Google, the game then opens a page to remove that link too.
  • Report and block: forum and chat posts have Report and Block buttons, and notices a Block button. Blocking stops that player's chat, requests and invites from reaching you. See our Community guidelines.
  • Access and portability: email us for a copy of your data.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Brazil), you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any right, email contact@mechitall.com; we answer within 30 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights.
