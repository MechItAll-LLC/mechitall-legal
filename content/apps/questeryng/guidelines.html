Be the kind of adventurer people are glad they met.

Be real

  • Use your own photos and your real age. You must be 18 or older.
  • Don't pretend to be another person, a business or Questeryng staff.
  • Keep one personal account.

Be kind

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, stalking or unwanted repeated contact.
  • No hate speech, or attacks on people because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age.
  • Take "no" for an answer, the first time.
  • Don't share anyone's private information (address, phone, workplace, photos or chats) without their permission, and don't try to find out where someone lives or is.
  • Don't record or photograph people at meetups without their consent, and don't post screenshots of private chats to shame or harass anyone.

Keep it appropriate

  • No nudity or sexually explicit content in photos, profiles, quests or activities.
  • No soliciting sex, or offering sex for money or Prestige.
  • Zero tolerance for anything involving minors. We ban these accounts and report them to the authorities. See our Child Safety Standards.
  • No graphic violence, and nothing that promotes self-harm.
  • No threats, and no bringing weapons to meetups, quests or activities unless the activity is a lawful, clearly described one where that's expected (for example a shooting range).

Keep it legal and honest

  • No selling drugs, weapons or other illegal goods and services.
  • No scams, phishing, pyramid schemes, or asking for money, gift cards or crypto.
  • No spam, and no advertising outside real activities, quests or organization jobs.
  • No farming Prestige with fake quests, no exploiting bugs, and no bots, scraping or extra accounts.
  • Don't use groups for spam, mass advertising, or recruiting into schemes.

Show up

  • Honor the quests and activities you take on. If plans change, say so.
  • Organizations: post real jobs and events with accurate details.

Groups and Tandem

  • Group names, descriptions, emblems and chats follow the same rules as everything else on Questeryng.
  • Don't post a closed group's invite link or code publicly. It's meant for the people you invite.
  • Hosts: be fair when you accept or decline join requests, and don't use a group to single out or exclude people because of who they are.
  • Parties: only show people in your party (its photo, name or size) who agreed to be in it, and keep party names and photos within these rules too.
  • Tandem is for practicing languages. Keep it about that: no flirting or romantic advances unless you've both opted into Dating and matched there.

What happens if you break the rules

  • Content can be hidden automatically when several people report it, or when our checks flag it. A moderator then reviews it.
  • Depending on how serious it is, we may remove content, send a warning, suspend your account for a period (read-only), or ban it permanently.
  • Serious or repeated violations lead straight to a ban.

If you think we made a mistake, contact contact@mechitall.com.
