The short version

  • Other people never see your location. We only ever show them a rough distance, like "under 1 km". Your coordinates stay on our servers.

  • We don't sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account). This deletes your data, as described below.

How others see you

Anyone signed in can see your public profile, the public parts of your status, your game progress (level, titles, Prestige), the languages you list, and the activities and quests you post. Nobody else ever sees your precise location, birth date, gender, dating preferences, private status fields, email address, phone number or push tokens. Nearby lists, the Meet deck and alerts show only a rough distance. Others can see your "Who can message me" setting. Hosts can limit an activity or quest to certain genders, an age range, classes or photo-verified adventurers; we check your private details only to decide whether you can join, and never show them to the host. If you join one that is limited, others in it can tell that you fit its limits. If you host activities, your profile shows how many people rated your events 4 stars or more on average (only once at least 3 have; never who, and never anything negative). When someone in one of your chats or groups looks for a fair meetup spot, our servers use the rough area (about 5 km) of each member who allows it to suggest public places roughly halfway; between two people the middle is also shifted by a fixed 1 to 2 km. Only the suggested places and a rounded "everyone within about X km" are shown, never anyone's location. You can turn this off in Settings → Help find fair meetup spots.

Two Merchant items share a little more on purpose: if someone uses a Lantern of Seeking, they can see the people who already liked them in Meet (and haven't been swiped on yet), so a like you give may be seen before you match; if someone uses a Summoning Scroll on you, you get a notification with their name.

Safety and moderation

To keep Questeryng safe, we check content automatically:

  • Photos: uploaded photos (profile, chat and event photos) are checked with Google Cloud Vision for explicit or violent content. Clearly explicit photos are removed; borderline ones stay blurred until the viewer taps them.
  • Text: reported content, new quests and activities, profile text, and the first message of a message request are checked with Google Cloud Natural Language, plus simple pattern checks for scams. A flagged message request goes to the recipient's "Filtered" folder instead of their requests.

Flagged content may be hidden and reviewed by our moderators. Moderators can also see content you report and your account's moderation history. When you report a chat message, or report someone from a chat, the last 20 messages of that chat (text, sender and time, and whether a message was a photo) are copied into the report so moderators can see what happened. This copy is kept with the report. We use rate limits and app integrity checks to prevent spam and abuse.

Meetup feedback and ranking

Meetup surveys are optional and anonymous. Nobody can see individual answers or anyone's exact score; when reviewing reports, our moderators can see a rounded summary of someone's standing, but never who said what; the person you rated is never told what you said. Once you've reached level 5 and met enough people (or joined enough activities) that no single person's feedback could stand out, you can see your own coarse level and the perks it gives you under "Your perks" on your profile. Your answers only change the other person's hidden standing, which is calculated automatically from ratings, confirmed meetups, no-show and fake-profile reports, how eager you are to meet (for example joining activities or applying to quests), and how recently you used the app. A higher standing means you appear earlier in other people's Meet deck, your quests appear higher on the board and your activities earlier in Explore, you get higher limits on posting, swiping and messaging, and you level up faster. A low standing has the opposite effect. Standing never hides you completely and is not used for anything outside Questeryng. Our moderators can see an account's standing and its totals (for example average stars and the number of no-show reports, never who gave them) when investigating reports. Contact us if you think your standing is wrong.

Who we share data with

We use these service providers to run Questeryng. They process data on our behalf.

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud (United States): sign-in, database, file storage, server functions, push notifications, crash reporting, analytics, app integrity checks, and the Vision and Natural Language moderation APIs. When someone taps Translate on a post, application, bio or chat message, that text is sent to Google Cloud Translation; translations are cached on our servers so the same text isn't sent twice.
  • OpenStreetMap: your device loads map images directly from OpenStreetMap's servers, which see your IP address and the area of the map you're viewing. To show places near you (parks, cafés, museums…), our servers ask OpenStreetMap's public Overpass servers for the places in a rough area of about 15 × 15 km around you; they never receive your identity or exact location, and the result is cached and shared by everyone in that area.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: to find out which country you're in, our servers send OpenStreetMap's public Nominatim service a location rounded to about 10 km, at most once per area (the answer is cached for about 6 months). It never receives your identity.
  • Ticketmaster: to import public events, our servers send Ticketmaster the approximate area of places where activities exist. We never send your personal data.
  • SeatGeek: when we import events from SeatGeek, like Ticketmaster, our servers send SeatGeek only the approximate area of places where activities exist, never your personal data. Imported events say which service they come from.
  • MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute, Norway): to show the weather forecast on an activity, our servers send MET Norway the activity's location rounded to about 10 km (the activity's place is already public), at most once an hour per area. It never receives your identity or your own location.
  • Google Calendar: if you tap "Add to calendar", your device opens Google Calendar with the activity's title, time and place; Google's own privacy policy applies there. We don't get access to your calendar.

We share information when the law requires it (for example a valid subpoena or court order), to protect someone's safety, to enforce our Terms, or to protect our rights. If MechItAll LLC or Questeryng is sold, merged or reorganized, your information may be transferred as part of that deal, and this policy will keep applying to it. We don't sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there's nothing to opt out of; we treat browser and device "Global Privacy Control" signals the same way.

How long we keep data

  • Your location is kept only as your latest position, and is deleted after 14 days without an update, or straight away if you turn off "Show me to adventurers nearby".
  • Verification selfies are deleted after review.
  • Waves are deleted after 7 days. Conversation activity is kept as long as the chat exists, and deleted with your account.
  • A declined message request deletes that chat for both people; the record that you declined it is deleted after 30 days.
  • Usage analytics are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months; crash reports for 90 days.
  • An accepted join request is deleted when you join. A declined one is kept, so it isn't sent again, until the group or your account is deleted.
  • Unanswered meetup surveys expire after 14 days. Meetup feedback counts less over time and is deleted with your account; ratings you gave others stay only as part of their anonymous totals.
  • Inactive accounts: if an account isn't used (no sign-in and no app use) for 2 years, it is deleted the same way as with "Delete account", after notifications 30 days and 7 days in advance; opening the app cancels it. Sign-ups that never created a profile are deleted after 30 days without use. Organizations that haven't opened the app for 90 days stop appearing in Meet until they come back.
  • A quest nobody takes expires 24 hours after its date, or 30 days after posting if it has no date.
  • Everything else is kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Access records (IP address and time of each app open) are kept for 6 months, also after an account is deleted, and then deleted.
  • Reports and moderation records may be kept after an account is deleted, to prevent abuse.
  • Cached translations are kept after an account is deleted. They contain only the translated text and its language, not who wrote it or who asked for it.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently deletes your profile, photos, private data, location, progress, meetup feedback about you, likes, matches, verification data and login. Your languages, Tandem preferences, messaging settings and restricted list, message requests, event photos you added, Merchant items, waves and conversation activity are deleted, you leave all your parties (a party left without members is deleted), and you leave all your groups: a group you host passes to another member (and stops being a guild), and a group with no members left is deleted. Your open quests and hosted activities are cancelled. In chats, your past messages remain for the other participants, labeled "Deleted adventurer". If you can't use the app, see Delete your account.
