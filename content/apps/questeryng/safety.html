Meeting new people should be fun. Here's how to keep it that way.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US) right away. Questeryng can't send help.

Before you meet

  • Chat in the app first. Keep conversations in Questeryng until you're comfortable. Reporting only works for things said in the app.
  • Look for the "Photo verified" badge. It means a reviewer matched a live selfie to that person's photos. It isn't a background check.
  • Protect your personal details. Don't share your home address, workplace, financial details or ID photos with someone you've just met.
  • Never send money. Don't send money, gift cards or crypto to anyone you meet on Questeryng, whatever the story. Report anyone who asks.

When you meet

  • Meet in public for the first few times: a café, a park, a busy venue or a group activity. In a chat, tap 📍 to find nearby spots marked "Good first-meetup spot", but always check a place yourself.
  • Tell someone your plans. Use Share my plans (on any quest or activity, or in a chat's ⋮ menu) to text a trusted contact who, what, where and when. You can set your trusted contact in Settings → Privacy & safety.
  • Get yourself there and back. Keep your phone charged, and be able to leave whenever you want.
  • Watch your drink and stay in control. It's fine to say no or change your mind at any time.

Side quests and jobs

  • Agree on what's involved, when, and any reward before you start.
  • For help at someone's home (moving, repairs), bring a friend or tell someone where you'll be.
  • Skip anything dangerous, illegal, or that needs a license neither of you has.
  • Legitimate jobs never ask you to pay upfront or to "buy equipment" first.

Groups and Tandem

  • Meet tandem partners in public, like a café, a library or a language meetup, especially the first few times.
  • For group meetups, check who's coming in the group chat, and leave any group that makes you uncomfortable.
  • If a closed group's invite link ends up somewhere it shouldn't, the host can make a new code from the group page. That cancels the old link.

Dating

  • Dating is only open to photo-verified adults.
  • Consent is required at every step and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • If something feels off, trust your instincts. Unmatch (chat ⋮ → Unmatch), block and report.

Your privacy tools

  • Location: other people only ever see a rough distance, never where you are.
  • Invisible mode: turn off "Show me to adventurers nearby" in Settings.
  • Your status: make any part of it private.
  • Blocking: blocked people can't message you or find you nearby, and their messages are hidden in group chats.

Reporting

Report a person from their profile (⋮ → Report), a quest or activity from its page, or a single message by long-pressing it. Reports are confidential. Content that several people report is hidden until a moderator reviews it. See our Community Guidelines.
